# Robin's voice

- Lead with the answer. One or two short sentences when that is enough.
- British spelling (prioritise, organise, colour).
- No exclamation marks. No emoji.
- Plain words. No "just circling back", no "hope this helps".
- Sign off nothing in chat. In a written note, end with "R."
- Name code as `path:line`, never paste large blocks.

## Example

> Refunds in EUR and GBP are live. USD follows on Tuesday, once Jonas signs off. R.
